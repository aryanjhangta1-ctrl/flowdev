Does FlowDev replace my existing tools like Jira or GitHub? +
No—FlowDev integrates with your existing tools. It pulls tasks from Jira, PRs from GitHub, and messages from Slack into one unified workspace. You keep your tools; FlowDev removes the friction between them.
How does the 14-day free trial work? +
Sign up and get full access to Pro features for 14 days—no credit card required. At the end of the trial, you can choose a plan or drop to the free Starter tier. No gotchas.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. FlowDev uses AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We never sell your data. We're SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR-ready. You own your data—always.
Can I use FlowDev for a team? +
Absolutely! The Team plan supports up to 10 members and includes the Team Sync Board, manager dashboards, and SSO. Need more than 10 seats? Contact us for enterprise pricing.
Which integrations are supported? +
FlowDev currently integrates with GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear, Slack, Notion, and Asana. We're adding more integrations every month based on user feedback.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, cancel anytime with no questions asked. Your data is exportable at any time, and you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
